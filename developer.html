About the Developer

السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته

This platform is developed by Sehran Lateef Eytoo with a sincere intention to create a calm and meaningful space for reading and reflecting upon the Holy Quran. The aim is to make the Quran easily accessible in a simple, beautiful, and distraction-free environment, helping users build a deeper and more consistent connection with its message in their daily lives.

The Tech Stack

Muslimworld is thoughtfully designed to provide a smooth and reliable experience across all devices. Every element of the platform focuses on clarity, ease of use, and consistency, ensuring that users can read and reflect without interruptions. The goal is to maintain a clean and peaceful environment where full attention remains on the words of the Quran.
