Our Aim

Connecting Hearts to the Quran

Our primary aim with Muslimworld is to provide a seamless, beautiful, and completely free platform for Muslims around the world to connect with the Holy Quran and the life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).

Accessibility First

We believe that access to the words of Allah should be barrier-free. That is why we have focused on creating an ad-free environment that works perfectly on any device, with multiple reciters and translations.

Sadaqah Jariyah

This project is built as a continuous charity (Sadaqah Jariyah). We hope that every letter read, every audio listened to, and every bookmark saved brings immense blessings to the users and the creators of this platform.
